# Corpus Communications

**Creative communication. Thoughtful design. Digital development.**

We are an independent communications agency based in Budapest, Hungary. Since 2000, we have helped businesses connect with their audiences through marketing, PR, design, and technology.

With our own creative, digital, and production team, we take projects from the first idea through development, launch, and ongoing support. We work closely with a select group of clients, building long-term relationships as part of their team.

## What we do

- **Web development** — Websites, online stores, custom design and development, hosting, maintenance, and search optimization.
- **Mobile applications** — Android and iOS apps, from interface design and backend integration to testing and publication.
- **AI and e-commerce solutions** — Tools for customer communication, sales workflows, product information management, automation, and business monitoring.
- **Marketing and communications** — Brand identity, digital campaigns, PR, media relations, print production, and corporate films.
- **Localization** — Translation and language services through [Lector](https://lector.hu/).
